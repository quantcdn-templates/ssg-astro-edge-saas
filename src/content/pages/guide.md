## Find your way around

**Overview** gives you a snapshot of the projects loaded in your workspace. **Projects** is where you search, filter and update your work. **Activity** shows the most recently updated projects. **Settings** lets workspace owners change the team name and description.

## Start something good

Choose **New project**, add a name and owner, then set a priority and target date. Break progress into a percentage your team agrees on. Save your changes to make them available to everyone in the workspace.

## Invite a fresh perspective

Ask your workspace administrator for a personal access key. Owners can manage projects and settings; viewers can explore the workspace without changing it. Each person should have their own key so access can be revoked independently.

## A calmer workflow

Use **Planned** for work on the horizon, **Active** for current work and **Complete** when it is ready to celebrate. Archive work you want to keep out of the active pipeline.
